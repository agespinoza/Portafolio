# Excel Data Cleaning Lesson

Teach learners to standardize station names, classify invalid records, and summarize valid inspection minutes.

## Career context

I taught practical Excel, VBA, and Access labs at Universidad Autónoma de Durango and designed technology curricula at other universities. This Excel exercise reflects that hands-on approach to teaching and assessment.
