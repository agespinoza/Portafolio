      SGP4 vs NASA ISS ephemeris

      Models, data and assumptions

      Data sources

      • Element sets: CelesTrak GP catalog, built from U.S. Space Force tracking data. A snapshot ships with the page; when it is older than a day the console downloads current sets and caches them for two hours.
      • Reference ephemeris: NASA Johnson Space Center (FOD/TOPO) ISS trajectory, CCSDS OEM v2, EME2000 frame, 4-minute state vectors.
      • Coastlines: Natural Earth 1:110m via world-atlas.

      Models

      • Propagation: SGP4/SDP4 (satellite.js 5.0, Vallado 2006 revision). Output in TEME, converted to geodetic with GMST.
      • Sun and eclipse: Astronomical Almanac low-precision solar position (about 0.01°) and a cylindrical umbra model. Penumbra is ignored.
      • Passes: geometric elevation above the selected mask, without refraction. Coarse search every 10–30 s, then bisection to 1 s for AOS and LOS.

      Validation

      • NASA's EME2000 vectors are rotated to the mean equator of date with IAU-1976 precession and compared with SGP4 in the radial, in-track and cross-track frame.
      • Nutation is neglected (under 0.7 km at ISS radius). The comparison uses the element set captured with the ephemeris, so it does not change when the elements are refreshed.
      • For education and portfolio use, not for operational decisions.
